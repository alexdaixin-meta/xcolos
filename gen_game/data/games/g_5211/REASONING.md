## Luck: 1 of 3

- match 2, turn 17, seat 1: "Green is my bonus colour, so I'll lead with my highest Green." (The bonus deal added marginal points in shared rounds.)

## Weak spots

- match 2, turn 11, seat 3: Added R3 to a Red count of 3, reasoning that a bust 'costs me nothing compared with them'. That is wrong: a bust also loses seat 3's own R1 points, and seat 2 made the same move at the same time. A non-Red card would have let Red score.
- match 1, turn 12, seat 4: Expected the others to back their own colours yet still played G2, which locked in a four-way tie. Backing a rival's colour would have made it the unique top and earned face value. The same mirrored failure happened in match 3, round 1.
- match 3, turn 72, seat 4: Counted on seat 1 to supply the third Kododo and ignored that seat 3 could also play its second K. Seat 3 at turn 71 likewise ignored seat 4. Five Kododos landed and the agreed 3/3 split failed for both of them.
