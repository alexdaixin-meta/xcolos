Game has 4 rounds. Each round: reveal 1 character card per neighborhood and draw 4 ship cards showing which colored gems are available in each neighborhood plus one market card that sets which gems are on market rows. Players bid two brokers face down to determine turn order; higher sum picks order first, ties broken by order cards. In turn order each player places 9 remaining brokers one by one onto board spots: either in a neighborhood's port, commercial, or palace area, or on the market intersecting a row and column. Brokers have values 0-4 and may be placed face up or face down to bluff. After placement, per neighborhood rank players by total broker value; top three gain gems from that neighborhood's ship in rank order, plus port majority gains a black gem, commercial majority gains instant points, palace majority gains the revealed character card. Market columns move price markers up/down, rows grant gems.
