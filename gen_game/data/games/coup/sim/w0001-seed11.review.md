## Gaps

### F1 [game_file, high] round 0 setup, all seats

- observed: deck_count announced as 12 for 3 players (15-card deck, 6 cards dealt). Remains 12 through rounds 1-14 after exchanges.
- expected: R1: deck should be 15 - 2×player count = 9. Spec State deck_count should be 9 and stay 9 when Exchange draws 1 and returns 1 (R19). Design SETUP/TURNS 8 says same.
- fix: Initialize deck_count = deck_size - hand_size*players and maintain R19: draw 1 / return 1 leaves deck_count unchanged; shuffle only if a card went back.

### F2 [game_file, high] rounds 1,2,3,4,5,6,9,10,12,13 reactions_to_the_action

- observed: On Tax (rounds 3-6,9-10,12-13) and Exchange (rounds 1-2) every other living player is offered ['pass','challenge','block_duke','block_contessa','block_captain','block_ambassador'] even though no block is allowed on Tax/Exchange. Foreign Aid/Captain/Contessa limits are ignored.
- expected: DESIGN step 3 / SPEC R6, R18: challenge only if role claimed; block_duke only vs Foreign Aid (anyone), block_contessa only vs Assassinate by target, block_captain/ambassador only vs Steal by target. Non-target seats must not be offered those blocks. No blocks on Tax/Exchange.
- fix: Filter reaction options per action and per seat: Exchange/Tax -> only pass/challenge; Foreign Aid -> pass/block_duke; Assassinate -> target only pass/challenge/block_contessa; Steal -> target only pass/challenge/block_captain/block_ambassador.

### F3 [game_file, medium] round 8, seat 1 (trace_lint)

- observed: Seat 1 was offered 'block_contessa' in reactions_to_the_action, picked it, engine refused and re-asked without it: 'the first offer held an option that was not allowed.'

### F4 [game_file, medium] rounds 11 and 14 target selection

- expected: SPEC R33 / DESIGN step 2: target must be another living player. Self-target must not be offered.
