### F1 [prompt, medium] rules brief; round 10, seat 1

- observed: The brief says: 'If the claim was true the claimer reveals it, shuffles it into the deck and draws a replacement, and the challenger loses a card.' It never says the action then goes ahead. In round 10 seat 1, with 1 hidden card, challenged seat 2's true Assassinate. Seat 1 took one loss for the failed challenge and a second from the assassination.
- expected: DESIGN step 4 says 'The action continues' and SPEC R21 says 'the action continues'. So challenging a true Assassinate costs the challenger a card and the assassination still lands, and a target with 2 cards can lose both (R27 covers the related double loss from a false Contessa block). Players need this to weigh a challenge. Seat 1 was out either way here, so this play shows no misread.
- fix: Add to the brief: 'a failed challenge does not stop the action: if it was an Assassinate, the target still loses a card.'

### F2 [prompt, low] rules brief; rounds 3, 4, 10 announcements
