A PREVIOUS VARIATION of this game was reviewed and tested. The findings:
Cut the choice steps per round to 4 or fewer.

1. Drop the passing round entirely. Each hand becomes 6 trick rounds, so 24 rounds in total. Remove pass_count and last_passing_hand, and set rounds_per_hand = 6 and total_rounds = 24.

2. Restructure each trick as two choice steps instead of four turns that rotate with the leader:

   - Then the other three players choose their cards at the same moment, as a simultaneous poll. They must follow the led suit if able, otherwise they may play any card.

3. Keep the leader rotation: player h leads the first trick of hand h, and the trick winner leads after that. Keep the reversis rule (0 for the taker, 15 to each other player) and lowest-total-wins.
