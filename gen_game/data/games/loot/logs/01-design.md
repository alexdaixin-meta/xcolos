Original game: Loot
Players in the original: 2 to 8

Original rules:
Shuffle the deck of merchant ships (values 2-8, 100 total), pirate ships in four colors with strength 1-4, four captains and an Admiral. Deal six cards to each player and place the rest as a draw pile. On your turn first claim any merchant ships you have won: your own unattacked ship from last turn, or any contested ship where you have held the highest total strength of one color (or the last captain/Admiral) since your last turn. Won merchants go face down for scoring; attacking cards are discarded. Then do one action: draw, play a merchant in front of you at sea, attack any merchant with a pirate of a new color (first attacker any color), add another pirate of your already-used color to boost, place your matching captain on your own pirate to trump everything, or place Admiral on your own merchant to defend. If multiple attackers tie, the ship stays. Game ends when draw pile is empty and a player plays his last card; unclaimed merchants are discarded and merchants left in hand subtract. Most net gold wins.
