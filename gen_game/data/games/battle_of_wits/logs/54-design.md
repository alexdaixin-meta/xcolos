Original game: Battle of Wits
Players in the original: 2 to 2

Original rules:
The game is for two players: the Poisoner and the Chooser. Two identical goblets of wine are placed on the table. In secret, the Poisoner decides which goblet(s) contain poison - in the classic version, exactly one goblet is poisoned, though the story version allows both. The Chooser then studies the opponent and selects one goblet for himself; the remaining goblet goes to the Poisoner. Both players drink simultaneously. If the Chooser drank poison he dies and loses; if he drank the safe goblet, the Poisoner dies and loses. In a variant, the Poisoner may be immune and wins regardless, making the choice purely about deduction and bluffing about who would poison which glass.

A PREVIOUS VARIATION of this game was reviewed and tested. The findings:
Playing the rules with numbers (every simple policy, 1200 games each) found:
- knowing your type does not pay: the best policy that uses it ('if Resistant->R, Sensitive->L', wins 19%, loses 19%) does no better than the best that ignores it ('always L', wins 20%, loses 18%)

- everyone playing 'if Resistant->L, Sensitive->R' (best joint play): mean total 10.6; every player tied in 100% of games, so 0% are decided by the draw
The variation that was played: Battle of Wits — Constitution Chalices: Setup: 2 players. 10 rounds numbered 1-10. Two goblets: Left and Right. Poison location is public deterministic: on odd rounds 1,3,5,7,9 Left is poisoned and Right is safe; on even rounds 2,4,6,8,10 Right is poisoned and Left is safe. At start each player is privately assigned a Constitution type: Resistant or Sensitive, each 50% independent. You know your own type, not the opponent's.

Each round:
1. Both players simultaneously and secretly choose a goblet to drink: Left or Right.
2. Choices are revealed.
3. Scoring for the round: If both chose the same goblet (both Left or both Right) both score 0 for the round (they clash, neither drinks cleanly). If they chose different goblets (one Left, one Right) the lone chooser scores points alone:
   - If the lone goblet was the poisoned goblet for that round: Resistant scores 3, Sensitive scores 1.
   - If the lone goblet was the safe goblet for that round: Resistant scores 1, Sensitive scores 3.
   The other lone player scores by the same rule for his goblet. Contested players score 0 regardless of type.
   Examples: Round 1 (poison Left): A Left alone, B Right alone -> A scores 3 if Resistant else 1, B scores 1 if Resistant else 3. If both Left -> both 0.
4. Totals accumulate to a running score. Proceed to next round.

No communication, no cards, no other hidden state. Winner: After 10 rounds the player with the higher cumulative total wins. If totals are equal the game is a draw. Winner is by arithmetic comparison only.
Do not tweak it: change the payoffs or the objective until every one of these problems is gone.

Revise the variation to fix these. Keep what works. Review it again against the criteria.
